## About us

The **AWES Laboratory** (Airborne Wind Energy Systems) at [Universidad Carlos III de Madrid](https://www.uc3m.es/inicio) develops science and engineering for **tethered airborne wind energy**: kites and rigid wings that harvest stronger, steadier winds at altitude, with power conversion on the ground. Our work spans **numerical modelling**, **control-oriented design**, **hardware-in-the-loop and bench validation**, **flight and campaign data**, and **energy management** — with a **system-level** view from simulation to field deployment.

We are one of Spain’s leading academic groups in airborne wind energy research and technology development.

> **Airborne Wind Energy (AWE)** uses lightweight flying devices instead of tower-mounted rotors, reducing structural mass while accessing higher-altitude wind resources — a promising complement to conventional wind power where portability, logistics, and performance matter.

Learn more on our public site: **[awes.uc3m.es](https://awes.uc3m.es)**

## Open engineering practice

This GitHub organization hosts the laboratory’s **version-controlled software**, **models**, **controllers**, **documentation**, and **structured metadata** that describe how digital assets relate to experiments and datasets. Heavy simulation outputs, raw campaign data, and large binaries remain on dedicated lab storage; repositories hold **source**, **schemas**, and **indexes** so work stays reproducible and discoverable for the team.

Repository families follow a consistent `awes-*` naming scheme (handbook, catalog, templates, domain-specific model and tool repos). **Most repositories are private** and intended for collaborators and students inside the group.
